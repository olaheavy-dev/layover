Starting a trip

From the first message to a confirmed brief. Layover asks for what is missing one question at a time, shows the whole brief for the traveller to check, and searches nothing until the traveller confirms. Each state is shown at 1440 px and on a phone, on the Chat tab and on the Trip tab.

1. The first message

The trip starts with the first message. Layover keeps what it was told, asks for the next missing detail (the length) with quick answers, and shows no brief until it has the whole of it.

1440 px
390 px, Chat tab
390 px, Trip tab

What to look at

  • The trip is already named, “Warm break, May”, from the feel and the month. The name never holds the budget or the number of travellers.
  • The canvas shows the small form of the welcome illustration and one line. No brief and no place are shown yet, so the phone has no trip strip.
  • One question only: how many nights. The answers are buttons in a named group, and “Something else” comes last.

2. The brief waits for your OK

Every field is on the canvas as a chip, with Confirm and Edit. Nothing is searched until the traveller confirms.

1440 px
390 px, Chat tab
390 px, Trip tab

What to look at

  • The chat says what it did with “two” and “about £800”: it states the reading, so the traveller can correct it.
  • Confirm and Edit are on the brief. The chat also offers “Yes, that’s right”, so a phone traveller can answer without leaving the Chat tab.
  • On a phone the trip strip says “Brief waiting for your OK”, and the compact card in the chat opens the brief.

3. Editing one part

The traveller taps “£800 in total”. It turns into a field. Nothing has been searched, so a change costs nothing.

What to look at

  • Only the budget is open for change. The other parts stay as chips.
  • The chat repeats that nothing is searched yet.
  • “Confirm brief” stays on the page, so the traveller can save and confirm in two steps.

4. Confirmed, and the comparison starts

The brief is confirmed. The Compare section opens with three cards that keep their names and show shapes until each place is researched.

What to look at

  • The brief says “Confirmed by you”, with Edit. The section index now lists Brief and Compare.
  • The three cards are loading: no figure, no photo and no price appears before it has a source.
  • The checks row in the chat is open while the checks run, and the hollow dots show work still going on.
