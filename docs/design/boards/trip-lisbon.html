The chosen place: Lisbon

The working trip after the traveller chose Lisbon. The brief and the comparison are folded to rows. The place leads with its photo, and the five key facts sit below it: fare, cost estimate, advice, weather and money. Each one shows its source and its age, as the label rule allows.

What to look at

  • Each card shows its label once, in its source line, when all its figures share one source and age. A price keeps its label beside it, and so does a row that differs from the rest of its card (the cost lines, the exchange rate). "Live" is written in words but drawn quieter than Cached.
  • Estimates are called estimates. The total says it is partial, and the budget sentence is in words.
  • The photo and the name lead. A tile band sits under the photo, and each card has a small heading above its title. In the dark theme, the photo throws a soft glow behind the page and the cards lift.
  • On a phone, the Trip tab shows the canvas and the section index scrolls sideways. The Chat tab holds the compact cards and the trip strip; see the chat board for it.
