Phone and navigation

Chat and Trip tabs on a phone with the “3 new” badge, the trip strip, the compact cards and the sideways section index; then the folded sections and the section index on a desktop.

1. Chat tab: trip strip, compact cards and the “3 new” badge

On a phone the chat and the trip are two tabs. The Chat tab keeps a strip above the message box and compact cards in the chat; the Trip tab says in words that something new is there.

1440 px
390 px, Chat tab

What to look at

  • The Trip tab says “3 new” in words, never a bare number. It goes away when the Trip tab is opened.
  • The strip above the message box shows the place photo, “Lisbon, May”, “5 facts ready” and View trip.
  • Each compact card has its figure and label, and View in trip takes the traveller to the full widget on the Trip tab. Each button’s name says which card it belongs to.
  • At 1440 px there are no tabs: the chat and the canvas sit side by side, so the badge, the strip and the compact cards are phone-only.

2. Trip tab: the sideways section index

The section index is one row at the top of the Trip tab. It has more sections than fit, so it scrolls sideways and keeps the current section in view. The first frame lands where View in trip on the Flights card leads; the second is further down, on Days.

390 px, after View in trip: Flights
390 px, scrolled to Days

What to look at

  • View in trip lands on the Flights widget, with its top just below the index, so nothing hides it. The index marks Lisbon as the current section.
  • On Days the row has moved: Brief is cut off on the left and Booking on the right, which shows that it scrolls. The current section is bold and underlined, so colour is never the only cue.
  • A section is in the index only when it has content. Pinned answers have no entry of their own: they sit at the end of the place section.
  • On the Trip tab, Brief, Compare and the place stay in order above Stay, Days and Booking.

3. Desktop: Brief and Compare folded

From 1024 px the chat is on the left and the canvas on the right, and both scroll on their own. Earlier sections fold to one row so the chosen place is on the first screen.

1440 px
390 px, Trip tab

What to look at

  • Brief is one row of chips with Edit. Compare is one line, “Compared 3 places: you chose Lisbon”, with Show again.
  • The section index has only three entries, because the trip has only reached the place. “Lisbon” is bold and underlined.
  • The folded rows keep their photos’ credits and every label stays visible, so nothing loses its source by being folded.

4. Desktop: Stay folded while on Days

Further on, the chosen place and the hotel fold too, so Days has the room. Each folded row can be opened again.

390 px, Trip tab

What to look at

  • Brief, Compare, Lisbon and Stay are one row each, in journey order. Days is open and “Days” is bold and underlined in the index.
  • Stay shows the chosen hotel by name and area, with Change. Layover has no hotel prices, and the row says so.
  • Booking is below the days, with “Sandbox: no real booking or payment”.
