Booking

Trying the flight booking in the sandbox: choosing the dates, waiting for approval, approved, declined and price changed. Booking is the last section on the canvas and has its own entry in the section index. The flow pauses and nothing is ordered until the traveller presses Approve. Every state says “Sandbox: no real booking or payment”.

1. Choosing the dates

The first step. Layover only has “May 2027, 5 nights”, so it asks for exact dates and never picks them. Nothing is searched or ordered yet.

What to look at

  • Layover asks “Which dates would you like to fly?” with date choices and “Other dates”. The choices are samples.
  • The Booking section says it appears once the dates are chosen: no price and no offer yet.
  • The sandbox label is already there: “Sandbox: no real booking or payment”.

2. Waiting for approval

The sandbox offer is in the Booking section. The chat says the flow is paused until Approve. On a phone the chat shows a compact card with “View in trip”.

What to look at

  • Approve and Decline are equal choices, both on the canvas. The chat has no Approve button, so nothing can be ordered by accident.
  • The sandbox label “Sandbox: no real booking or payment” is the first thing in the widget and on the compact card.
  • The price, £192 for 2, has its Live label and says it is a sandbox price, not the fare above. The dates are the ones the traveller chose in step 1.

3. Approved

After Approve, the sandbox order runs and the widget shows its result: SANDBOX-0001.
