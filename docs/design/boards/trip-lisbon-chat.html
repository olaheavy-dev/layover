The long chat and the phone cue

The Lisbon trip after a long chat. On a phone, the Chat tab shows the conversation with each answer's checks folded into one row, compact cards for what joined the trip, a trip strip above the message box and a "3 new" badge on the Trip tab. On a desktop, the chat is the left column next to the trip.

What to look at

  • The checks for one answer are one row, "4 checks · Show". Tap it to see the steps.
  • Scroll up in the chat: "Jump to latest" appears. The compact cards have scrolled off the top by now, which is why the trip strip stays above the message box.
  • The strip names the trip and what is ready ("5 facts ready"). The Trip tab says "3 new" in words, not just a number.
  • The sea temperature answer names its source (Open-Meteo Marine) and says Live. Prices on the compact cards keep their label beside them.
