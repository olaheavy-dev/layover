Chat-only replies and interruptions

What happens in the chat without the canvas changing: a web search answer, an honest “I can’t help with that yet”, an answer the traveller stops, a message that is not about travel, a lost connection, and the New trip and Share trip steps. All of these are in the page: no browser alert and no browser dialog.

1. Web results in the chat

The question is a travel question that Layover has no tool for, so it runs a web search. The result stays in the chat.

What to look at

  • The widget says “From a web search, not checked by Layover” at the top, with the retrieval date. It never uses Live or Cached, and it has no “Add to trip”: a line under it says so.
  • The two quotes come from example.org and are marked “sample quote”. The boards never put made-up words in a real site’s mouth.
  • The canvas does not change. On a phone the widget shows in full in the chat, so the Trip tab is not needed.

2. Stopping an answer

While an answer streams, the Send button turns into Stop. The traveller can end the answer at any point.

1440 px
390 px

What to look at

  • Stop has a word and an icon, not only an icon, and it sits where Send was.
  • The check row says what is still running. A hidden line tells a screen reader that Layover is answering.
  • The caret is still: nothing moves, so reduced motion needs no special case.

3. I can’t help with that yet

The web search found nothing usable. Layover says so and says what it can do. No widget.

390 px

What to look at

  • The line is plain and one sentence. It does not guess an answer.
  • The list of what Layover can do gives the traveller a way on.
  • Nothing is added to the canvas.

4. Not about travel

A message that is not about travel is declined politely in one line, with an example. No trip is made, so this is the welcome screen with the decline in the chat.

What to look at

  • One line: the decline and an example in the same sentence. No lecture.
  • The canvas still says “Your trip” and shows the illustration: no trip was made, and the top bar has no trip name and no Share trip.
  • The four example prompts stay under the decline, so the traveller can pick one.

5. Lost connection

The message could not be sent. It keeps its text and is marked “Not sent”, and the chat offers Try again.

1440 px
390 px

What to look at

  • “Not sent” is a word and an icon on a dashed outline: the traveller’s text is not lost.
  • The notice is in the chat, uses the warning colour with words and has one action, Try again. It is not a browser alert.
  • The canvas stays readable: it shows what was there.

6. New trip

New trip asks for confirmation inside the page and says what happens to the current trip.

1440 px
390 px

What to look at

  • It says the current trip stays at its working link. It makes no promise about how long, because how long links last is not decided yet.
  • Cancel and Start new trip are two clear choices with words. The scrim shows the page is waiting.
  • On a phone the dialog sits at the bottom, where a thumb reaches it.

7. Share trip

Share trip makes a frozen, read-only copy without the chat, and shows its link with a Copy button. A tick box, unticked, asks whether to include the budget and who is travelling.

What to look at

  • It says what the copy is: frozen, read-only, no chat, “as of 18 Nov 2026”. The box “Include my budget and who’s travelling” is unticked, and one line says what ticking it adds.
  • The link is a sample (layover.example/s/sample-share-link) and the dialog says so.
  • Copy is a button with a word. Done closes the step.
