Layover boards · Before a trip has a place

Vague and unclear messages

What Layover does when a message is missing a detail, is unclear, names a place that has more than one match, or asks a side question before any place is chosen. It asks one question at a time and never guesses.

1. A detail is missing

Layover asks one short question, with the likely answers as buttons. The canvas shows what it knows and what is missing, never a guess.

390 px

What to look at

  • The brief on the canvas has dashed "not set yet" chips. "Not too pricey" is shown in the traveller's words, with "no amount yet": it is not a budget.
  • The trip is named "Warm break, May", from the month and the feel.
  • Only one question is asked. The later ones wait.

2. The meaning is unclear

Layover asks one question and offers what it can do as choices, instead of guessing.

390 px

What to look at

  • The choices are buttons in a named group. "Something else" is always last.
  • No trip exists to show yet, so the canvas is a quiet line of text. The illustration comes after the illustration test.

3. Which Valencia?

A place name with more than one match gets a "which one" question. Layover never picks one itself (BRIEF-03).

What to look at

  • Both matches are real places, and the board says nothing else about them.
  • "Somewhere else" covers a third Valencia, or a different place.
