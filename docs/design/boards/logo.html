Layover boards · Logo

The Layover logo: the suitcase character

The idea: a cheerful little suitcase, ready to go. Friendly, classic travel. The body and the word use the brand blue (--logo-ink), and the sun sticker uses the lighter marker colour (--logo-sun). Use the switch to see the dark theme.

The lockup

The mark and the word, as they sit in the top bar. In the top bar the lockup is 32 px high from 1024 px, and 26 px high from 640 px.

The mark and its small form

The full mark has a handle, two straps, a smile, wheels and the sun sticker. The small form drops the straps and the wheels and makes the face, handle and sticker bolder, so it still reads at 16 px.

One colour

The files in logo/ are one-colour masters: they take the colour of the text round them (currentColor). The face, straps and sticker ring are cut out of the body, so nothing else is needed. Here they are in the page text colour and reversed on the brand blue.

Clear space and smallest size

  • Clear space. Keep a margin round the logo equal to the height of the suitcase body (the box without the handle and wheels), divided by two. Keep text, edges and other marks outside it.
  • Smallest size. Lockup: 24 px high. Full mark: 32 px high. Small form: 16 px high. App tile: 32 px. Below 32 px, use the small form.
  • Colour. Body and word in --logo-ink, sun in --logo-sun. In one colour, use currentColor and never a pale colour on white (3:1 or more against the background).
  • Do not stretch it, turn it, change the sun to another colour, or put it over a photo without a plain panel behind it.
  • Phone. Below 640 px the top bar shows the small form alone, 28 px high; the link keeps the name "Layover" for screen readers.
