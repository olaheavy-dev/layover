Comparing

Three places side by side after the brief is confirmed: loading, filled with a “Not known” row, one failed card, a swapped place and no place fits. On a phone the cards are one row that scrolls sideways, on the Trip tab; the Chat tab has the trip strip and a compact card.

1. Loading: one place ready

Each card fills in when its research finishes. Lisbon is ready, Valencia and Valletta keep their names and show shapes.

What to look at

  • Lisbon shows figures with their labels (Cached, 3 days old, and Live for the weather). The other two show no figure at all.
  • The grouped checks row is open and says what is done and what is still running.
  • On a phone the cards sit in one row that scrolls sideways and snaps to each card. The strip says “Checking 3 places, 1 ready”.

2. All three filled, with a “Not known” row

Valencia’s stay says Not known, so its known costs are partial and “Against £800” is also Not known. Nothing is shown as £0.

390 px, Chat tab

What to look at

  • Valencia’s stay and “Against £800” rows are in the “Not known” style, with the reason. Layover does not guess a stay price.
  • Each card has Choose. The Trip tab on the phone shows a “3 new” badge while the Chat tab is open.
  • The chat says in words what the card shows in the table.

3. One card failed

Valletta’s card could not load. It says so and offers Try again. The other two stay as they were.

What to look at

  • The failed card has no figure and no photo, and uses the warning colour with words: it is never shown as a low or free price.
  • Try again is on the card only. Choosing Lisbon or Valencia is still possible.
  • The checks row marks the failed check in words.

4. A place swapped for the traveller’s own

The traveller swapped Valletta for Porto. The card says “Researching your place”, and shows no figure and no photo for it.

What to look at

  • Porto’s card is named by what the traveller typed. Its dashed outline and “Your place” tag set it apart from the places Layover chose.
  • “Swap back to Valletta” is on the card. Nothing about Porto is known until its research finishes.
  • Lisbon and Valencia keep their figures while Porto loads.

5. No place fits

It says which part of the brief rules places out (the budget, marked on its chip) and offers Edit brief.

What to look at

  • The budget chip is marked “rules places out”, in words as well as colour. The other parts stay plain.
  • Edit brief is the one main action. No card, price or photo is shown, so nothing looks like an answer.
  • The brief above stays folded to one row, with its own Edit.
