Proposed widgets

Proposals for issue #26: four widget kinds that the journey spec does not list yet. Public holidays, time difference, fares to anywhere and a TfL journey to the airport, each drawn in every state that applies. Plan 1 fixes the schemas.

What to look at

  • Sources. Holidays and the time difference come from offline sources (the Python holidays library and the IANA time zone database). Fares come from Travelpayouts and the airport journey from TfL.
  • Labels. The two offline kinds reuse Cached with a release date. TfL is Live. Both are marked proposed, because the brief’s table has no row for them.
  • Nothing made up looks real. Every holiday is “Sample holiday”. Fares, ages and journey times are made up and listed in sample-data.md.
  • Not known and Empty. A missing fare is a row that says Not known, never zero. No fares at all is an Empty card that says what Layover looked for.
  • Phone. All four leave a compact card in the chat, at the end of the gallery.
