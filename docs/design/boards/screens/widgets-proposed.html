Proposals

These four kinds are not in the journey spec’s list yet. They are proposals for issue #26. The names, sources and labels are for the owner to approve, and plan 1 fixes the schemas. A label marked proposed is not in the brief’s Source labels table.

Each kind with the states that apply: filled, loading, error, not known, and one extra where the kind needs it. The caption above a card is for the gallery. It is not part of the widget.

public_holidaysPublic holidaysProposed

The holidays that fall in the trip month at the destination (the trip dates, once they are set).

Proposed kind
public_holidays
Shows
The public holidays that fall in the trip month at the destination (the trip dates, once they are set), each with its date, its name and where it falls in the trip.
Source
Python holidays library, offline, MIT, no terms of service (data sources, “Free APIs chosen for integration”). Nager.Date is a candidate that was not chosen (commercial use needs sponsorship).
Label
Cached, release of 1 Nov 2026 (sample date) proposed. The brief’s table has no row for an offline library. Cached with the dataset’s release date is the nearest existing label (the Foursquare row works the same way).
Needs
A country (and a region, if local holidays should count) from the place. The dates here are made up: Layover could not run the library for these boards, so every holiday on this page is “Sample holiday”.

time_differenceTime differenceProposed

How far ahead of or behind the UK the place is on the trip dates.

Proposed kind
time_difference
Shows
How far ahead of or behind the UK the destination is on the trip dates, in words, with the two zone names. It says “Same time as the UK” when there is no difference.
Source
IANA time zone database, offline, public domain, read through Python zoneinfo (data sources, “Free APIs chosen for integration”). The tzdata package would need approval as a dependency.
Label
Cached, release of 1 Nov 2026 (sample date) proposed. Same reasoning as public_holidays.
Needs
The place’s time zone (from its coordinates or country) and the trip dates, because the difference can change when clocks change. The two figures here were checked with Python zoneinfo on 20 Nov 2026 (see sample-data.md).

fare_listFares to anywhereProposed

The cheapest cached fares from one airport in a month, to several places.

Proposed kind
fare_list
Shows
The cheapest cached fares from the traveller’s airport in a month to several places (“to anywhere”), lowest first, each with its own age and a link to check the live price. It is the vague-brief answer to “where can I go cheaply?”.
Source
Travelpayouts Aviasales Data API: cheapest fares, price calendars and popular destinations, cached for 2 to 7 days (data sources, Chosen).
Label
Cached, 3 days old, already in the brief’s table. It shows on every row, because each fare has its own age and a price always has its label beside it.
Needs
The sort is done by tested code, never by the model. The widget never says a fare is cheap or “cheaper than usual”, because it has no comparison to name (bar rule 3). Fares, places and ages here are made up (sample-data.md).

transport_routeTransport to the airportProposed

A London journey to the airport: its steps and how long it takes.

Proposed kind
transport_route
Shows
The steps and the total duration of a public transport journey in London to one airport (Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted or London City), as the TfL Journey Planner returns it.
Source
TfL Unified API, free key, “Powered by TfL Open Data” and the Ordnance Survey credit must show; 500 calls a minute per feed (data sources, “Free APIs chosen for integration”).
Label
Live proposed. TfL has no row in the brief’s Source labels table: the answer is fetched for this request, so Live fits.
Needs
A start point in London. The 43 min total is the three steps added by hand (5 + 32 + 6); the real total comes from TfL, not from the model. Stations are real places, but every time here is made up (sample-data.md).

Compact cards in the chat (phone)

All four kinds can be on the canvas, so each leaves a compact card in the chat on a phone: its icon, its title and its label. A tap opens it in the Trip tab.
