Pages outside the trip

The shared trip, How Layover works, a link that does not work, and the limit-reached state.

1. Shared trip

What a friend sees at the shared link: the trip, frozen and read only. Every age carries the date the trip was shared, so an old fare is never read as a new one.

1440 px
390 px, top
390 px, the facts

What to look at

  • The header says “Shared trip, as of 18 Nov 2026” and that fares and advice can change after that date.
  • There is no chat, no message box and no Edit, Choose, Add to trip or Share trip. “Plan your own trip” leads to the welcome screen.
  • Every Live and Cached label adds “as of 18 Nov 2026”. A “Not known” label has no age, so it stays as it is.
  • The FCDO advice keeps its own “updated” date and its link.

2. How Layover works: what it does and the agent team

One page for every visitor, and for recruiters in particular. The first two sections say what Layover does and show the team of agents.

1440 px
390 px, top
390 px, the team

What to look at

  • The team diagram is HTML, so a screen reader reads it as an ordered list. The caption says the same in words.
  • The slot for the mood scene is a designed placeholder, because the owner has not made the image yet. It will carry the “Illustrative photo:” alt text and the footer line when it exists.
  • On a phone the four steps stack in order. The section index at the top scrolls sideways, like the one on the Trip tab.

3. How Layover works: rules, data sources and evaluation

The six rules in plain words, each data source with its terms, and the evaluation section, which says “No evaluation results yet” until scores exist.

390 px, the rules

What to look at

  • The rules and the sources come from decision 0011 and the data sources list. Nothing new is claimed.
  • Each source has a link, what it gives and its terms. Open-Meteo’s non-commercial terms are stated, not hidden.
  • Evaluation shows no score, no number of trips and no comparison, because none exists. The label is in the “Not known” style.
  • The last section links to the GitHub repository.

4. Link not found or expired

A wrong link, or one that has passed its time limit, looks the same: the page does not say whether a trip ever existed.

1440 px
390 px

What to look at

  • The page says the link does not work and gives two reasons it may be so, without saying which applies, and without saying whether a trip ever existed.
  • “Plan a new trip” is the one main action. The small isometric illustration is the welcome scene without its ticket, and is decoration only.
  • There is no trip name in the top bar, because there is no trip.

5. Limit reached

The traveller has reached a limit. The message box is turned off and a notice says so. The trip stays readable and can still be shared.

1440 px
390 px, Chat tab
390 px, Trip tab

What to look at

  • The notice has no figure, because the limits are not set yet. It says when planning returns in words: when the limit resets.
  • The input and the Send button are disabled, and their text says why. Share trip is still in the top bar.
  • The chat and the canvas stay as they were: the traveller can read everything and keep the link.
