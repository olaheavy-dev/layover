Place widgets

Nine kinds, each with every state in the widget state table that applies: filled, loading, error (Unavailable), not known, and the extra states such as an over-budget estimate, the chart as a table, the Wikipedia fallback and the map as a list. Each card is captioned with its state. The caption belongs to the gallery, not the widget.

What to look at

  • Loading is placeholder shapes in the form of the widget. The shapes sweep softly, and stand still when the traveller asks for less motion. Each loading card is marked busy and carries a hidden "Loading" line for screen readers.
  • Error says "Unavailable" in words, with an icon and a diamond, so colour is never the only cue. It says what could not be checked, shows no figure, and offers "Try again" where a retry helps. The advice card keeps its FCDO link.
  • Not known is quiet italic text with an empty dot. It says what is missing, and it never shows a zero.
  • One label per card, prices always. A card shows its label once, in its source line. Fares and cost lines keep their label beside the price.
  • Partial and over budget. A partial total says it is partial and does not compare itself with the budget. An over-budget total says so in words.
  • An inland place gets no sea card at all, drawn here as a dashed note, not a card.
  • The map is drawn for the board with no map tiles and is marked "Sample map". If the real map cannot load, the places show as a list with links.
