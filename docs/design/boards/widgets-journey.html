Journey widgets

The widgets a traveller meets from the brief to the booking: your brief, compare places, where you’re staying, days, try booking, web results and “not known”. Each kind shows every state in the state table: loading, empty, error, not known and its own other states. Scroll inside the frames. The 1440 px frame shows the gallery in a column about 720 px wide.

What to look at

  • Loading is placeholder shapes in the form of each widget. The shimmer stops when reduced motion is on, and the widget says it is busy to a screen reader.
  • An error says “Unavailable”, what failed and Try again. It never shows a zero, an old figure as current, or a favourable value.
  • A missing value is “Not known” in quiet italics with an empty dot: a row in the comparison (Valencia’s stay), a hotel’s stars and website, one stop’s opening times, or the whole answer.
  • Try booking always carries “Sandbox: no real booking or payment”. Nothing is ordered until Approve, and a changed price asks again.
  • On a phone the comparison is a row of cards that scrolls sideways and snaps, with the same rows in the same order.
  • Web results live in the chat, never get “Add to trip”, and carry their own label. A label shows once for each card, and always beside a price.

Figures and names come from sample-data.md, in “Widget gallery: journey widgets” for the new ones. The frames are live pages: scroll inside them.
