Stay and Days

Where the traveller sleeps, and the days built from it: a hotel chosen, a hotel skipped, a hotel changed, and a trip that began as an itinerary request with no comparison. The Stay section comes before Days, because the days start from the hotel or from the centre.

1. Hotel chosen, days built from it

Sample Hotel Alfama is the base. Day 1 and Day 2 start from it, Day 2 has a stop with no opening times, and Day 3 is empty.

What to look at

  • The chosen hotel says “Your base”, and the other two offer Choose instead. Stars and website say Not known where the source has nothing: nothing is guessed, and there are no prices.
  • Day 2: Torre de Belém says “Opening times: Not known”, and Mosteiro dos Jerónimos still shows 10:00 to 17:30. The row is not hidden.
  • Day 3 says it has too few sourced places and offers Look in a wider area or Leave it free. The afternoon is not filled with places that have no source.
  • The map above now shows the hotel pin. The section index ends Lisbon, Stay, Days.

4. Itinerary only: “Three days in Lisbon”

No brief and no comparison. The canvas starts at the place, then Stay, then Days. The trip name is “Lisbon”.

What to look at

  • The section index is Lisbon, Stay, Days. There is no Brief and no Compare, because nothing was briefed or compared.
  • The place shows only its introduction and the map. Fares, costs, weather and daylight need an airport, a budget or dates, and nobody has given any, so they are not shown (the agent’s call, see the note for the owner).
  • The information architecture’s example is Porto, but the sample data has no Porto days, so the board uses Lisbon. The trip name has no month, because none was given. The brief’s naming rule does not cover this case yet (a gap, see the note for the owner).

Notes for the owner

  • Sea card on the side-answer screens. In the real planner a coastal place would already have its sea card, so the beach question could point to it instead of adding a second one. The boards leave the sea card out of the canvas on the two side-answer screens, so it is not drawn twice. Decision needed: does “Add to trip” stay on an answer the canvas already holds?
  • Itinerary-only trip name. The brief names a trip by place and month. A request such as “Three days in Lisbon” has no month, so the board shows “Lisbon”. The rule needs a line for this case.
  • Itinerary-only place. The agent chose to show only the introduction and the map there. Say if the traveller should also see the currency or the weather.
