What to look at

2. A side answer in the chat

The traveller asks “What’s the time difference with Lisbon?”. Nothing on the canvas answers it, so the answer is a new widget inline in the chat, with its source and label, and an Add to trip button.

What to look at

  • The answer is the time difference card: “Same time as the UK” for 15 May 2027, the middle of the month (the dates are not set yet), labelled Cached (release of 1 Nov 2026, a sample date), with its source. The words say there is no clock change to make.
  • Add to trip is a quiet button under the answer, and the line beside it says where the card will go. The canvas has no such card yet, so adding it is a real choice.
  • The canvas is the approved one, with the sea card in its place. The time difference card is a proposed widget, drawn on the “Widgets: proposed” board.

What to look at

  • The answer is the sea card: 17°C, Live, Open-Meteo Marine. The words add the typical highs and rainy days, with their own source line. Layover gives the facts and says the choice is the traveller’s.
  • The line under it says “Already in your trip” with a View link. View opens the sea card on the canvas, or the Trip tab on a phone. Nothing is added twice.
  • The decision of 20 Nov 2026 (see the register in the decisions README): an answer the canvas already holds never offers Add to trip.
